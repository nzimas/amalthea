Screens

The Brick's 1024 × 768 screen as Amalthea draws it: straight to the framebuffer, in its own typeface (Oxanium), with no UI toolkit.

Amalthea

Start

The boot screen: the horn of plenty. Every start is blank and stopped.

Amalthea

Home

The instruments: TRACKER, FX and LIFEBLOOD. The battery charge top right.

Tracker

Pattern

Five columns, sixteen rows (or eight larger ones). The track number, large, in its colour.

Tracker

Editing a note

Every edit is shown large and auditioned at once; nothing is stored until Enter. F1 / F2 browse the note's tabs: length, condition, pan, volume, five sends.

Tracker

An effect on the FX column

WAVEFOLD on FX 1, its dry/wet mix and its LENGTH: the effect keeps its slot over the next four rows.

Tracker

Selecting rows

FUNC + F1 / F2 selects rows; Enter edits all of them at once, ERASE clears them.

Tracker

Track settings

Length, clock, direction, instrument, volume, pan and the sends, per track.

Tracker

Master effects

ABRASION, CORROSION, MODULATION, compressor, delay, reverb: values in real units.

Tracker

Session settings

Tempo, scale and root (with a die for a new pair), and when queued patterns switch.

FX

The rack

One strip per FM-1 track: two insert slots, level and bypass.

FX

Slot sequencer

Sixteen steps per slot, in two rows; the lit step follows playback.

FX

Parameter locks

A step's own values for the slot's effect; unlocked values follow the slot's defaults.

FX

Generating a sequence

DENSITY and COMPLEXITY; hold X to generate triggers and locks.

Lifeblood

The field

Six FM-1 tracks enter on the left; vessels carry them through five effects to OUT.

Lifeblood

A node

Its effect and parameters: X draws another effect, Y new values.

Lifeblood

Session settings

PATH and CHARACTER choose the recipe a network grows from.

Lifeblood

Snapshots

Thirty-two per session: tap Bank to recall, hold to store.

Amalthea

Power

Hold Power and release: the prompt starts on NO, and warns about unsaved changes.
